# Write-Blocker Validation Practice

**Status:** Supporting source note for forensic-technology portfolio content  
**Last updated:** July 17, 2026  
**Related file:** [`FORENSIC_TECHNOLOGY_CHRONOLOGY.md`](./FORENSIC_TECHNOLOGY_CHRONOLOGY.md)

## Documented practice

All mounted and portable forensic write blockers used in Troy's digital-forensics workflow were tested and validated at least annually to confirm proper operation and continued write-protection performance.

## Why it matters

This experience supports truthful claims involving:

- forensic-laboratory quality control
- evidence-integrity safeguards
- defensible acquisition procedures
- validation rather than assumption of hardware performance
- chain-of-custody discipline
- procedural consistency
- readiness for audit, testimony, peer review, or technical scrutiny

## Permitted wording

- `Used mounted and portable hardware write blockers in forensic acquisition workflows and ensured they were tested and validated at least annually for proper write-protection performance.`
- `Supported defensible forensic acquisition practices through at least annual validation of mounted and portable write blockers.`

## Guardrails

Do not claim laboratory accreditation management, sole ownership of the validation program, formal calibration authority, or a specific validation standard unless supporting documentation is located.

## Evidence to locate when available

- annual validation logs
- test forms or checklists
- forensic-lab SOPs
- equipment inventory or maintenance records
- photographs showing write-blocker models
- training or task-force documentation describing validation practices
