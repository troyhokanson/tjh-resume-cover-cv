## Purpose

Prioritize public-safety vendors and role families where official public evidence shows that former law-enforcement officers, detectives, crime-scene personnel, digital-forensic examiners, instructors, or military veterans have moved into comparable work.

An incumbent biography proves that a transition path exists. It does **not** prove why that person was hired, that the same employer will repeat the decision, or that copying the person's wording will win an interview. Use the pattern to improve evidence selection, not to imitate a person or manufacture causation.

## Product-fit watchlist without pathway points

CentralSquare, Genetec, Skydio, BRINC, DroneSense, RapidSOS, Peregrine, NICE, OpenText, Exterro, X-Ways, SUMURI, Griffeye, and similar vendors remain legitimate targets when the posting fits. They receive **no employer-pathway bonus** until official incumbent or program evidence is verified.

This distinction prevents a familiar product from being mistaken for a demonstrated hiring preference.

## Role-family evidence selection

### Implementation and professional services

Lead with:

- Genetec AutoVu agency-side implementation and workflow adoption.
- Training, curriculum, FTO, and user adoption.
- CAD/RMS, Axon, forensic, evidence, and patrol-to-investigation workflows.
- Coordination among users, command staff, IT, prosecutors, vendors, and partner agencies.

Keep formal vendor-side SaaS implementation years, configuration ownership, integrations, and API work as explicit gaps when required.

### Customer success and technical account work

Lead with:

- User empathy grounded in real operational pressure.
- Technical-to-nontechnical teaching and adoption.
- Long-term stakeholder credibility and multi-agency coordination.
- Product-specific end-user evidence and a concrete example of changing a workflow or teaching a user.

Do not claim renewals, churn targets, QBR ownership, account portfolios, CRM fluency, SLAs, or quota responsibility without direct evidence.

### Solutions consulting and pre-sales

Lead with:

- Discovery: identifying what an officer, detective, examiner, supervisor, prosecutor, or IT partner needs from the workflow.
- Demonstration and instruction evidence from FTO and adjunct teaching.
- Direct product/operator knowledge and the ability to explain operational consequences.
- Agency-side scoping, procurement, grants, implementation, and adoption from the AutoVu project.

Treat formal enterprise-sales cycles, RFP ownership, CRM pipeline management, quota, APIs, and integration architecture as separate requirements.

### Training and enablement

This is one of Troy's most direct vendor lanes. Lead with the combined pattern: practitioner credibility + adult instruction + curriculum + field coaching + forensic/evidence depth. For Magnet, Cellebrite, LexisNexis, Axon, and CAD/RMS vendors, choose the product and workflow evidence that matches the course or customer audience.

### Forensic customer delivery

Lead with examiner workflow, chain of custody, tool progression, validation, reporting, courtroom/prosecutor communication, and teaching. Magnet is the closest public incumbent benchmark because its training biographies show the same combined pattern of military service, detective work, computer examination, FTO, and instruction.

### Field deployment and program delivery

Use Army service only where the duties matter:

- 95B Military Police: public-safety mission and field-operation familiarity.
- 11B and 19K: safety, accountability, adaptability, and mission execution.
- 88M: fleet, logistics, deployment, and equipment workflows.

The combined military contribution is capped. It cannot override missing software implementation, UAS, electrical, networking, or people-management requirements.

## Computation order

1. **Hard eligibility:** work authorization, approved geography, remote/hybrid status, mandatory clearance/license, compensation, and travel.
2. **Requirement fit:** direct, transferable, unsupported, and unknown evidence for each material requirement.
3. **Transition calibration:** apply the role-family weights in the JSON matrix. Cap all transition signals at 12 points and all military signals at 3.
4. **Employer pathway:** add no more than 3 points, and only when the verified employer pathway overlaps the actual role family.
5. **Gap control:** hard blocks and major gaps still govern the recommendation.
6. **Fit zone:** Direct, Near, Targeted Stretch, or No-Go. Do not report a false interview probability.

Protected traits always contribute zero points and are handled only by the separate application self-identification standard.

## What successful transition profiles consistently do

The official biographies show a repeatable presentation pattern:

1. Translate former service into the employer's customer problem.
2. Name a concrete operational workflow, product, deployment, investigation, or training responsibility.
3. Show progression or ownership rather than relying on the badge, rank, branch, or MOS alone.
4. Pair credibility with communication, training, adaptability, or customer outcomes.
5. Make technical gaps bounded and learnable instead of hiding them.

Troy's materials should mirror that evidence structure—not another person's language.

## Interview-conversion defaults

- Lead the summary with the target business outcome: implementation, customer adoption, technical discovery, training, or forensic customer delivery.
- Use `former detective`, `digital-forensic examiner`, `crime-scene team member`, or `Army veteran` as credibility after the business outcome is clear.
- Put one product/workflow proof point in the top third of the resume.
- Use one quantified scope metric only when it is verified and directly relevant.
- Address the largest likely objection in the cover letter: explain agency-side implementation as transferable and state what vendor-side ownership has not yet been held.
- Prioritize employee referral or transition-advice outreach to incumbents with a similar background, but never imply a personal relationship or ask a stranger to endorse skills they have not observed.
- Apply within the first viable posting window with a role-specific resume; speed never overrides evidence verification.
