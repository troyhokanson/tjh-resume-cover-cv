# Case Evidence Review Standard

## Purpose

This standard governs how Troy Hokanson case evidence is reconciled before any case fact, role description, metric, resume bullet, cover-letter example, interview story, portfolio statement, or public professional claim is used.

The goal is to preserve accuracy, evidence hierarchy, role precision, and privacy. Never substitute a plausible inference for an exact source match.

## Automatic Trigger

Apply this standard whenever a case involves or may involve:

- an Electronic Crimes Task Force (ECTF) or similar task-force referral;
- digital-forensic examination;
- electronic devices, cloud records, social-media records, email, or online accounts;
- preservation requests;
- search warrants or legal process for electronic evidence;
- ICAC or child-safety referrals;
- digital-evidence reporting or technical support to another investigator.

## Mandatory ECTF Crosswalk

For every ECTF-related case review:

1. Search the exact agency case number across every available ECTF workbook.
2. Search every year tab, not only the year of the agency incident.
3. Search filtered examiner or Hokanson views in addition to full-year tabs.
4. Search separate forensic-examiner case inventories when available.
5. Record each exact `YYLA...` match and the source workbook, tab, and row in the private Career Evidence Master.
6. Treat a full-year row and a filtered-view row as duplicate views when all identifying fields point to the same underlying matter.
7. When more than one distinct exact ECTF number is found, retain every verified number and explain the separate intake, evidence, or processing event.
8. When no exact match is found, record `Unresolved—Not Found`, list every source searched, and continue looking for primary evidence.
9. Never manufacture, extrapolate, or infer an ECTF number from an agency case number, incident date, or year.

## Year Rule

The year prefix in an ECTF `YYLA...` number reflects task-force intake or processing, not necessarily the year of the underlying agency incident.

A case opened by the agency in one year may receive its ECTF number in a later year because devices, warrants, provider returns, or forensic processing were received later. The exact source record controls.

## Role Verification

ECTF workbooks are reliable for crosswalking identifiers, dates, agencies, intake records, and examiner assignments only to the extent supported by the row.

Do not treat a workbook's `Investigator`, `Examiner`, `Lead`, or similar field as conclusive when it conflicts with:

- a contemporaneous forensic report;
- a case report or warrant record;
- a verified assignment record;
- direct clarification from Troy;
- another higher-quality primary source.

Separate the primary investigator's role from Troy's specialist digital-forensic, technical-consultation, preservation, warrant-support, referral, examination, and reporting duties.

## Evidence Hierarchy

Use evidence in this order when available:

1. Contemporaneous forensic reports, examination notes, warrants, orders, and official case records.
2. Original ECTF or forensic-examiner workbooks.
3. Independent court, prosecutor, or government outcome records.
4. Retrospective summaries and later work samples.
5. Direct factual clarification from Troy, clearly labeled and dated.

Record conflicts rather than silently blending them.

## Continuous Review and Exception-Only Pause Rule

Once a case-review batch is authorized, continue through all routine, reversible steps without asking for separate approval at each stage. Routine work includes:

1. locating and opening likely source files;
2. searching exact identifiers and date variants across available workbooks and indexes;
3. comparing duplicate or derivative records;
4. reconciling Troy's role against the evidence hierarchy;
5. assigning verification, sensitivity, retention, and public-approval status;
6. updating the private Google Drive Career Evidence Master and reading back the write;
7. updating sanitized GitHub standards or reusable career facts when supported;
8. updating the Notion command center, case record, or exception status when available;
9. continuing to the next reviewable item in the active batch.

Pause only when one of these conditions applies:

- a file deletion, permanent replacement, public release, publication, or application submission is requested;
- two high-quality primary sources conflict and the conflict cannot be resolved from available evidence;
- a decision depends on Troy's personal preference rather than an evidence rule;
- the candidate, case, role, or target record cannot be identified safely;
- the proposed action could expose protected names, identifiers, victim information, or other sensitive material;
- a connector, validation, or write action fails in a way that could leave systems inconsistent.

When a pause condition occurs, do not stop the entire batch. Record an exception with the blocked item, reason, sources searched, safest provisional status, next evidence target, and exact user action required. Continue processing other independent items that are not blocked.

After each meaningful batch, provide a completion receipt identifying what was reviewed, what changed in GitHub, Google Drive, and Notion, what failed, what remains unresolved, and whether any user decision is required.

## Cross-System Ownership

- **Google Drive:** private evidence, source files, hashes, exact internal crosswalks, duplicate control, and the Career Evidence Master.
- **GitHub:** sanitized standards, verified reusable career facts, validation logic, and application-generation rules. Do not store protected case identifiers or private evidence.
- **Notion:** human-readable command center, project status, application status, review queue, exceptions, next actions, and links to the authoritative Drive and GitHub records.

A system is not considered updated until the connector confirms the write and the result is read back when the tool supports readback.

## Privacy

Agency case numbers, ECTF numbers, names, victim information, account identifiers, device identifiers, warrant details, raw evidentiary paths, and sensitive offense facts are internal record-matching data.

Do not place them in resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn, public GitHub content, portfolios, or interview language. Public-facing material must use sanitized methods, role, scale, and outcome language only after approval.

## Completion Gate

An ECTF-related case is not fully reconciled until the private Career Evidence Master records either:

- an exact verified ECTF crosswalk with source workbook, tab, and row; or
- `Unresolved—Not Found` with every searched source documented.

The case must also have a verified role statement, evidence hierarchy, privacy status, and public-approval status before use in application materials.
