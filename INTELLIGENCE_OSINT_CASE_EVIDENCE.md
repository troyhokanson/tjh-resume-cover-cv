## All-source intelligence positioning

Across multiple real investigations, Troy repeatedly used an all-source investigative cycle:

1. Develop leads from public sources, commercial databases, digital evidence, human reporting, and transactional data.
2. Corroborate identities, accounts, relationships, locations, and behavior across independent sources.
3. Identify evidence-loss and operational risk early.
4. Preserve volatile platform/cloud evidence before subjects are alerted when appropriate.
5. Use lawful provider process and forensic examination to move beyond open-source leads.
6. Integrate physical, video, financial, geospatial, provider, and device evidence into a defensible operating picture.
7. Translate findings into warrants, reports, briefings, referrals, and prosecution support.
