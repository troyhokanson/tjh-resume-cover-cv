## Boundaries

- Do not copy all 1,238.75 documented hours into a resume.
- Route only role-relevant training.
- Distinguish course completion, credential, certification, and tool exposure.
- Suppressed or PTSD-sensitive training remains excluded unless the role explicitly requires it and the applicable gate is opened.
