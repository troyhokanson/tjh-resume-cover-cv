# Investigations and Casework Evidence

Routing archive for privacy-safe investigative methods, outcomes, and quantified examples. `CASE_BANK.md` remains the canonical detailed source.

## Intended use

- SIU and fraud investigations
- Financial-crime and program-integrity analysis
- Corporate investigations and security
- Digital-forensics and intelligence roles
- Interview preparation and STAR examples

## Boundaries

- Follow `PRIVACY_STANDARD.md` and suppress names and unnecessary identifying details.
- Keep unrelated cases and outcomes separate.
- The BEC restitution figure remains pending confirmation and must not be used as a confirmed BEC outcome.
- Suppress PTSD-sensitive cases unless a narrowly appropriate role and explicit gate permit use.
