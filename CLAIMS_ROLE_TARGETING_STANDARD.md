# Claims Role Targeting Standard

Use this standard before routing any claims, claims-audit, claims-quality, fraud-referral, or adjuster posting into the `siu-fraud` lane. It prevents two equal errors: rejecting a viable transferable role because the posting uses insurance language, and overstating Troy as an experienced adjuster or carrier-claims professional.

## Verified Transferable Evidence

Troy may be positioned around:

- 25 years of law-enforcement investigation, documentation, interviewing, and independent case management
- accident and loss-scene investigation
- resolving conflicting accounts against physical, digital, financial, and documentary evidence
- fraud-indicator recognition and referral-quality factual reporting
- litigation and prosecutor preparation
- customer communication developed through public service, teaching, and residential real estate
- training, coaching, and quality review developed through 19 years as a Field Training Officer

These are transferable capabilities. They are not evidence of carrier claim ownership.

## Unsupported Claims Experience

Unless a later verified source changes this standard, never claim:

- prior insurance-carrier employment or ownership of a claim inventory
- coverage interpretation or coverage decisions
- reserving, settlement authority, settlement negotiation, or payment approval
- proficiency in Guidewire, ClaimCenter, Xactimate, carrier estimating tools, or another claims platform
- medical coding, utilization review, clinical review, or healthcare-claims adjudication
- workers' compensation administration
- property-construction estimating or roof inspection
- an active adjuster license

## Classification Gates

### Direct

Use `siu-fraud` as a direct match when the primary work is fraud investigation, suspicious-loss review, evidence development, interviewing, legal-process coordination, fraud referral, or investigative reporting and the posting accepts law-enforcement or equivalent investigative experience.

### Transferable

Retain for review, with a documented experience-gap penalty, when the employer provides training and the role is one of the following:

- Claims Investigator or Claims Fraud Investigator where carrier claim ownership is secondary
- Claims File Auditor, Claims Quality Auditor, or Claims Quality Analyst centered on evidence sufficiency, documentation quality, investigative procedure, compliance, or litigation readiness
- Investigation-led Claims Review Specialist
- Claims Adjuster Trainee or Associate Claims Adjuster where licensing may occur after hire and prior carrier experience is not required
- trainee-level auto, general casualty, liability, or property-damage work that accepts investigative, legal, public-safety, military, or customer-facing experience

### Reject or Apply a Major Penalty

Reject when a hard requirement cannot be met. Apply a major penalty when the requirement is strongly preferred and central to the work:

- active adjuster license required at application with no obtain-after-hire or reciprocity path
- multiple years of direct claim ownership, coverage decisions, reserving, negotiation, settlement authority, or carrier-system experience
- complex bodily injury, workers' compensation, medical-record auditing, clinical review, coding, or utilization review
- Xactimate, construction estimating, roof or ladder inspection, catastrophe deployment, or heavy field-territory travel
- premium auditing centered on payroll, accounting records, classifications, or onsite business audits unless explicitly trainee-level

## Context Is Not a Requirement

Do not reject a posting merely because it mentions adjusters, underwriters, attorneys, providers, medical records, carrier systems, or Xactimate as collaborators or surrounding context. Evaluate what the candidate must personally know or do at entry.

## Application Language

For transferable claims roles:

- state the gap plainly: Troy is moving from investigation into carrier claims work
- connect verified evidence to the actual duties, not to generic insurance vocabulary
- describe licensing and carrier systems as learnable requirements only when the employer provides a path
- never call Troy an experienced adjuster, claims auditor, claims handler, or insurance professional
- use only verified case outcomes from `CAREER_CONSTANTS.md` and `CASE_BANK.md`

## Decision Record

Each fit assessment must record:

- classification: Direct, Transferable, or Reject
- hard requirements met and unmet
- licensing timing
- direct carrier-experience requirement
- physical or travel demands stated in the posting
- the exact transferable evidence used
- the largest hiring-manager concern
