## Role purpose

Manage strategic relationships with Axon Justice's largest prosecutor and public defender customers. Learn each customer's workflows and organizational structure, guide implementation and adoption, establish success plans, resolve escalations, and help customers use the Axon Justice platform more efficiently and confidently.

## Principal responsibilities

- Maintain regular customer calls, business reviews, and support for daily needs.
- Monitor adoption metrics across the Axon Justice platform.
- Create success plans and document progress toward customer goals.
- Coordinate multiple internal teams during critical escalations.
- Communicate major milestones and issues to senior leadership.
- Work with marketing and training teams on webinars and customer calls.
- Develop deep product knowledge and support proper implementation.
- Share justice-industry practices that address customer needs.
- Partner with Sales on account growth and expansion opportunities.

## Required background

- Deep justice or legal-sector experience, ideally in a prosecutor or public defender office.
- Knowledge of discovery, digital evidence management, case management, motions and filings, courtroom processes, and law-enforcement interaction.
- Direct engagement with prosecutors, district attorneys, assistant district attorneys, public defenders, justice IT, and related legal practitioners.
- Familiarity with statutory deadlines, disclosure duties, and ethical obligations.
- Strong project management, process improvement, organization, presentation, and communication.
- Experience tracking product adoption or engagement through data-analysis tools.
- Ability to manage change and high-stress situations.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.

## Application-specific questions

The official application asks candidates to confirm justice-sector experience, hands-on discovery or digital-evidence familiarity, willingness to travel 30-35%, stakeholder groups directly supported, and justice workflows personally performed.

### Sr Customer Success Manager - Justice

Oregon-Remote; San Francisco, California, United States; Scottsdale, Arizona, United States; Washington, United States

Join Axon and be a Force for Good.

At Axon, we're on a mission to Protect Life. We're explorers, pursuing society's most critical safety and justice issues with our ecosystem of devices and cloud software. Life at Axon is fast-paced, challenging and meaningful. Here, you'll take ownership and drive real change. Constantly grow as you work hard for a mission that matters at a company where you matter.

### Your Impact

The Justice Senior Customer Success Manager will work with Axon Justice's largest Prosecutor and Public Defender customers to build long-term, strategic partnerships. You will work closely with your clients to understand their workflows, organizational structures and overall requirements. As their main point of contact, your customers will lean on you to provide best practices and to help them achieve their operational goals through the use of Axon's products.

You will also work closely with multiple cross-functional teams by providing invaluable insight into customers' needs and challenges, helping influence and guide internal product road-maps and projects.

As someone who operate in a cross-functional capacity with a particular focus on Justice workflows and stakeholders, from project management to product expert to consultant, you will continually be focused on making your customers more efficient and more confident within their daily workflows.

### What You'll Do

**Location:** Remote, West Coast  
**Reports to:** Director, Customer Success  
**Travel:** 30-35%

- Engage with your customers through regular calls, business reviews and daily needs
- Monitor and manage adoption metrics to ensure customers are utilizing the full Axon Justice platform
- Create success plans for your customers and document customer progress toward established goals and results
- Manage escalations closely, acting as the quarterback for all critical issues and ensuring multiple internal teams are aligned
- Communicating major milestones and updates to Sr. Leadership on a regular basis
- Work closely with Axon's marketing and training teams to conduct regular training webinars and calls
- Develop a deep understanding of Axon's product lines and ensure proper implementation in every customer's environment
- Learn and share industry best practices in order to solve customer needs
- Partner with Sales to identify growth and expansion opportunities within accounts

### What You Bring

- **Required Experience: Justice / Legal Sector.** We are seeking candidates with deep experience in the justice/legal sector, ideally within Prosecutor or Public Defender offices, with strong knowledge of discovery, digital evidence management, case management, motions and filings, courtroom processes, and interactions with law enforcement. Experience working with prosecutors, defense attorneys, or other legal practitioners is highly valued.
- **Proven Justice Stakeholder Engagement:** The role requires proven ability to engage effectively with justice stakeholders (e.g. DAs, ADAs, PDs and IT). Candidates should be familiar with the constraints and priorities these stakeholders face (e.g. statutory deadlines, discovery disclosure, ethical obligations) and able to translate those into customer success strategies.
- Strong project management skills with a proven system for tracking multiple customer requests, issues and strategic plans
- A proven track record of leading process improvements within large organizations
- Previous experience tracking product adoption and customer engagement through data-analysis tools
- Self-motivated, team-oriented, responsible, and focused on exceeding customer expectations
- Exceptional organizational, presentation, and communication skills, both verbal and written
- Demonstrated ability to deal with change and excel in high-stress situations
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience
