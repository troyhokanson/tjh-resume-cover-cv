## Role purpose

Manage strategic relationships with Axon Justice's largest prosecutor and public defender customers. Learn each customer's workflows and organizational structure, guide implementation and adoption, establish success plans, resolve escalations, and help customers use the Axon Justice platform more efficiently and confidently.

## Principal responsibilities

- Maintain regular customer calls, business reviews, and support for daily needs.
- Monitor adoption metrics across the Axon Justice platform.
- Create success plans and document progress toward customer goals.
- Coordinate multiple internal teams during critical escalations.
- Communicate major milestones and issues to senior leadership.
- Work with marketing and training teams on webinars and customer calls.
- Develop deep product knowledge and support proper implementation.
- Share justice-industry practices that address customer needs.
- Partner with Sales on account growth and expansion opportunities.

## Required background

- Deep justice or legal-sector experience, ideally in a prosecutor or public defender office.
- Knowledge of discovery, digital evidence management, case management, motions and filings, courtroom processes, and law-enforcement interaction.
- Direct engagement with prosecutors, district attorneys, assistant district attorneys, public defenders, justice IT, and related legal practitioners.
- Familiarity with statutory deadlines, disclosure duties, and ethical obligations.
- Strong project management, process improvement, organization, presentation, and communication.
- Experience tracking product adoption or engagement through data-analysis tools.
- Ability to manage change and high-stress situations.
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.
