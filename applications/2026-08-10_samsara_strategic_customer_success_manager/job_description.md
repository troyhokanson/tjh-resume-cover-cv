## Role purpose

Work with Samsara's largest fleet customers after the Services handoff, understand their operating challenges, advise them on how to use the Connected Operations platform, and remain their long-term strategic partner.

## Primary responsibilities

- Help customers improve operational safety, efficiency, and sustainability through Samsara's IoT platform.
- Compose joint success plans that define objectives, metrics, timelines, and barriers to business value.
- Orchestrate executive business reviews with customer decision-makers and Samsara executives.
- Conduct operational workshops and recommend workflow changes that improve product use.
- Explain the platform to customers in field services, utilities, transportation, school transportation, and other physical-operations sectors.
- Coordinate with Product, Support, Finance, Sales, and Sales Engineering on customer requests.
- Mentor other Customer Success team members.

## Minimum requirements

- Six or more years of work experience, with the most recent experience in senior Customer Success, account management, or strategic consulting.
- Experience supporting Enterprise SaaS and/or Fortune 500 companies.
- Strong priority management and emotional intelligence in a high-visibility role.
- Experience supporting or working with technical products.
- Strong problem-solving and solution-development skills.
- Evidence of trust-building, communication, and change leadership with executives, Product and Engineering leaders, and daily software users.
- Initiative and effectiveness in a fast-moving, change-heavy environment.
- Diplomacy, tact, and poise under pressure.
- Bachelor's degree from a four-year institution.
- Ability to travel 25-40%.

## Preferred qualifications

- Strong bias for action and high standards.
- Experience supporting large-scale business solutions at Fortune 500 companies.
- Ability to work effectively in an unstructured, fast-moving environment.

## Company and product context

Samsara provides a Connected Operations cloud platform for organizations with physical operations. Products include video-based safety, vehicle telematics, apps and driver workflows, and equipment monitoring. The role serves large fleet customers and depends on adoption, workflow change, executive alignment, cross-functional coordination, and measurable customer outcomes.
