## Role Summary
Sibylline is hiring an embedded intelligence Team Lead to support a global technology client. The role leads a small analyst team covering the Americas and Europe while remaining hands-on in complex assessments, investigations, monitoring, reporting, and stakeholder support. The Team Lead is expected to improve the maturity of the intelligence program, establish priorities and escalation paths, quality-check written products and briefings, and work closely with the client's Security Manager and Sibylline support functions.

## Core Responsibilities, Paraphrased
- Lead and coach a small embedded analyst team; set priorities, delegate work, and maintain quality.
- Partner with security stakeholders to define intelligence requirements, tasking, escalation rules, incident support, and responses to information requests.
- Oversee all-source monitoring, including online and social signals, for emerging threats, incidents, reputational risks, and trends.
- Produce and quality-check concise intelligence products and verbal briefings for operational and leadership decisions.
- Maintain and improve SOPs, definitions, monitoring workflows, incident-support processes, and reporting practices.
- Support program maturity through simple metrics, review cadences, stakeholder feedback, and service improvements.
- Support onboarding, coaching, development planning, performance management, and coverage coordination with HR.
- Personally handle complex assessments, priority investigations, and high-visibility stakeholder requests.
- Coordinate with Sibylline operations, global intelligence, technology, and training resources.

## Requested Background, Paraphrased
- Experience with intelligence programs related to operational security, event security, activism-related threats, or geopolitical risk is preferred.
- Strong all-source research, OSINT collection, triage, escalation, writing, editing, briefing, stakeholder management, and analytical judgment.
- Evidence of people leadership, coaching, quality assurance, SOP/workflow improvement, and program-performance discipline.
- Familiarity with commercial monitoring tools such as Dataminr, AI-assisted tools such as Google Gemini, and Google Workspace is preferred.
- Bachelor's degree in a relevant field such as Criminal Justice; master's degree preferred.
- At least five years of relevant corporate intelligence, security intelligence, geopolitical risk, or adjacent operational-security experience.
- At least three years of team leadership or management, including coaching and quality assurance.
