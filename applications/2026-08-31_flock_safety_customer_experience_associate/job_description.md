## The Problem

As Flock continues to expand across communities, delivering rapid, high-quality support to our growing customer base is essential to keeping local safety systems running smoothly. Managing diverse inbound inquiries across live chat, phone, and email requires dedicated problem-solvers who can navigate technical challenges with speed and empathy. You will work directly to resolve customer inquiries, partner cross-functionally to address escalated issues, and contribute to self-serve resources that keep our operations scaling effectively.

Required: Must be comfortable working weekends.

## What You Will Own

- Resolve inbound customer inquiries efficiently across phone, email, and live chat channels with high accuracy and care.
- Perform technical troubleshooting on software and hardware interactions to identify root causes and deliver lasting solutions.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Engineering, Sales, and Customer Success teams to resolve complex customer escalations.
- Maintain a personal support queue and manage ongoing cases to ensure clear, timely updates to internal and external stakeholders.
- Author and update articles in the Customer Help Center to expand self-service resources and decrease recurring ticket volume.

## What This Role Is Not

- This is not a passive or script-reading support job. The associate actively investigates technical issues and drives cross-functional resolutions.
- This is not a strict Monday-through-Friday role. Weekend shifts are required to support operational needs.
- This is not a single-channel position. Phone, live chat, and email communications are handled interchangeably.

## What You Bring

- Demonstrated success in customer-facing roles. Technical support, hospitality, or public-safety dispatch experience is identified as a strong fit.
- Proven ability to troubleshoot software and hardware issues while communicating technical solutions clearly to non-technical users.
- Skilled written and verbal communication across email, phone, and live chat.
- Adaptability to manage competing priorities independently and navigate changing operational needs in a fast-paced environment.
- Experience with tools such as Salesforce or Jira is a plus.
- Required: Must be comfortable working weekends.
