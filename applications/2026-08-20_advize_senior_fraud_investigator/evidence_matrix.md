## Selected evidence ranking

1. Occupational fraud case: closest direct match to charge validation, financial-record review, Excel analysis, defensible documentation, restitution, and prosecutor handoff.
2. Multi-victim BEC case: strongest proof of complexity, pattern analysis, cross-jurisdiction coordination, quantified loss, and recognized report quality.
3. Self-initiated patrol fraud follow-up: strongest proof of independent case ownership and follow-through without close supervision.
4. DCECTF evidence operations: strongest proof of large-volume analysis, prioritization, confidentiality, and clear reporting across stakeholders.
5. Remote adjunct faculty work: strongest proof of sustained remote discipline and the ability to explain technical and regulatory concepts clearly.
