## The Opportunity

SandboxAQ is seeking an experienced Background Investigator / Analyst to join its Information Security team and insider threat program. The investigator will partner with Human Resources, Information Security, and Legal to identify, evaluate, and quantify personnel risk. The work includes comprehensive analytical reports that substantiate risk determinations.

The successful candidate will independently manage investigations from intake through final reporting, identify relevant risk indicators, assess source credibility, and provide actionable recommendations to executive stakeholders. The role requires sound analytical judgment and an understanding of privacy, employment screening, and personnel security considerations.

## Key Responsibilities

- Use open-source intelligence and commercial investigative tools for comprehensive research and information gathering.
- Verify the accuracy, completeness, and reliability of information from available sources.
- Perform quantitative risk analysis through a consistent and repeatable methodology.
- Partner with Human Resources, Legal, and Information Security to identify, triage, and mitigate internal and external personnel security risks.
- Handle sensitive personal data with discretion and escalate high-risk or ambiguous findings to leadership.
- Refine investigative methods, risk-scoring models, research procedures, and reporting standards as the personnel risk program matures.
- Conduct research and analysis under applicable laws, regulations, company policies, and ethical standards.

## Essential Skills and Experience

- Bachelor's degree in Criminal Justice, Criminology, Psychology, Intelligence Studies, or a related field, or equivalent work experience.
- Three to five years of professional experience in background investigations, due diligence, employment screening, corporate investigations, intelligence analysis, or related investigative work involving U.S.-based people or organizations.
- Proficiency with investigative research databases and tools, or demonstrated ability to learn commercial research platforms quickly.
- Strong written communication and the ability to synthesize complex findings into clear risk assessments and dossiers.
- Understanding of privacy regulations and ethical standards for background investigations and employment screening.
- Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple investigations with accurate documentation and consistent standards.
- Discretion and sound judgment with confidential, sensitive, or potentially adverse information.
