Dear Meta Hiring Team:

Twenty-five years in Minnesota law enforcement and prior U.S. Army service taught me how to work when the facts are incomplete, the consequences are real, and a decision cannot wait. I am applying for Meta's Oversight Operations Investigator role because the core work is familiar: triage a high-priority escalation, connect evidence across systems, coordinate the people who can resolve it, document the reasoning, and improve the process so the same failure is less likely to recur.

As a Lakeville detective and digital forensic examiner, I handled sensitive investigations that required independent judgment, careful legal process, technical evidence review, and clear communication with prosecutors, agency leaders, federal partners, businesses, and other investigators. I served as a digital forensics resource within a ten-agency task-force structure and personally processed 5,304 GB of evidence in 2020. In a wire-fraud matter, I performed a write-blocked forensic acquisition, examined the image with FTK and Internet Evidence Finder, identified responsive artifacts, and produced report media for the police case and the Minnesota Commerce Fraud Bureau.

The value I would bring to Global Response Operations is disciplined escalation judgment. I know how to separate signal from noise, test competing explanations, identify what the evidence supports, and state what remains unknown. I have written search warrant affidavits, investigative reports, case summaries, timelines, and leadership briefings for audiences who were not there for the investigation. I also spent 18 years as an adjunct criminal justice faculty member and nearly 20 years as a Field Training Officer, which required turning judgment, policy, and hard-earned experience into practical instruction, review standards, and repeatable decision frameworks.

AI is already part of my daily work. I use multiple AI systems for research, prompt development, workflow design, document review, and structured quality checks. I compare outputs against source material, correct unsupported claims, and keep consequential decisions under human control. Through GitHub, Notion, and AI-assisted workflows, I have built reusable instructions, audit rules, and document systems that make complex work more consistent. I would not claim formal production-agent deployment or enterprise red-team ownership that I have not yet performed. I can claim active proficiency, a skeptical review mindset, and the ability to translate expert judgment into explicit rules and practical workflows.

Meta's highest-risk escalations affect real people, public trust, and the reliability of systems used at enormous scale. That work deserves mature judgment, factual restraint, and clear accountability. My record shows that I can move a difficult matter from an uncertain first report to a documented and reviewable resolution while helping others understand how the conclusion was reached.

Respectfully,
