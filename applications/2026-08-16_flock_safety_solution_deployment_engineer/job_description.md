## Role Summary

The Solution Deployment Engineer sits on Flock's presales deployment team and owns technical customer deployments from discovery and solution design through implementation, troubleshooting, quality review, and handoff. The role works directly with customers and across Sales, Product, Operations, Customer Experience, and field teams.

## Core Responsibilities

- Design and execute technical deployment solutions for customer engagements across the United States.
- Translate customer requirements, process gaps, and success criteria into workable deployment plans.
- Support deployment strategy from pre-sales through implementation.
- Troubleshoot technical problems in real time and develop practical alternatives when obstacles arise.
- Document best practices, deployment methods, and lessons learned.
- Coordinate cross-functionally with Sales, Product, Operations, Customer Experience, and Field Operations.
- Maintain product knowledge and provide technical guidance related to license plate readers, live video, and future product releases.
- Provide Product teams with field feedback that can improve tools, internal processes, and deployment methods.
- Help resolve technical or administrative obstacles that could delay successful deployment.

## Candidate Profile

- Two or more years in technology presales, solution engineering, deployment engineering or equivalent work, or criminal justice.
- Crime-prevention or crime-mapping experience is a plus.
- Hands-on technical delivery in customer-facing work.
- Strong troubleshooting ability and calm performance under pressure.
- Strong communication and presentation skills with technical and non-technical audiences.
- Comfortable with shifting priorities and a fast-moving environment.
- Consultative problem solver focused on customer needs.

By approximately 60 days, the engineer is expected to complete deployment reviews independently, manage complex exceptions, work with Field Operations on quality control, and own assigned relocation-review work.
