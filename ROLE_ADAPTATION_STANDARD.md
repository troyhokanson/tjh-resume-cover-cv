# Role Adaptation and Authoring Autonomy Standard

**Purpose:** Give an AI writer enough freedom to produce a strong, industry-native resume and cover letter for the actual role, while keeping facts, privacy, and credibility locked.

This standard applies to every resume, cover letter, CV, recruiter packet, and professional bio. It controls how the other repository standards are interpreted.

## Governing principle

**Lock the facts and safeguards. Adapt the presentation.**

The AI must not force one master resume, one opening sentence, one vocabulary set, or one document structure across different industries. It should first analyze the job posting and employer, select the closest role lane, and then make professional writing and formatting decisions consistent with current practice in that lane.

## What is locked

These requirements never change:

- Every claim must be true and traceable to Troy's verified experience, education, training, case outcomes, or tools.
- Never invent metrics, titles, certifications, software proficiency, responsibilities, dates, or customer outcomes.
- Apply the privacy and PTSD-scope hard blocks.
- Omit the entire POST / peace-officer licensing credential and all licensing identifiers.
- Do not expose case subjects, victims, internal case numbers, badge numbers, or private identifiers.
- Be honest about developing skills and credentials in progress.
- Produce an ATS-readable primary resume.
- Apply `RESUME_HEADLINE_SCANABILITY_STANDARD.md` to non-federal resumes.
- Match keywords only when Troy can support them.
- Preserve Troy's natural voice: direct, specific, grounded, and human.

## What the AI may decide

After reviewing the complete job posting, the AI has discretion to choose:

- The wording of the role-aligned headline and opening sentence, within the headline standard.
- Which parts of Troy's background receive prominence and which are shortened or omitted.
- Resume section order, section names, bullet structure, and summary style.
- Whether a summary, selected achievements section, technical skills section, core competencies section, or project section improves the document.
- Industry-standard terminology from the posting.
- The amount of law-enforcement, military, teaching, real-estate, customer-facing, and technical detail.
- The most relevant verified outcomes and metrics.
- Cover-letter narrative, paragraph structure, degree of warmth, and professional closing.
- A plain ATS header or the branded navy/gold presentation header.
- One-page or two-page length based on seniority, relevance, and content density; two pages are acceptable for Troy's experience when both pages are relevant.
- Whether a portfolio or sanitized work sample should be referenced.

There is no mandatory detective-led opener. There is no requirement to mention military service, law enforcement, teaching, or a case metric in the first sentence. Those assets are used when they strengthen the match.

## Required workflow

1. Read the entire posting and identify the role's primary outcomes, audience, tools, seniority, and industry.
2. Select one primary lane below. Note a secondary lane only when the role is genuinely hybrid.
3. Identify the five to eight strongest verified matches and the most important gaps.
4. Choose an industry-appropriate headline, resume architecture, and vocabulary.
5. Draft freely within the truth, privacy, and scanability boundaries.
6. Run the ATS, privacy, and factual verification gates.
7. Read the final document as a hiring manager in that lane. Remove terminology imported from an unrelated lane.
8. Record the selected lane and rationale in the build log.

## Role lanes

### 1. SIU and insurance investigations

**Typical roles:** SIU Investigator, Senior SIU Investigator, Insurance Fraud Investigator, Field Investigator, claims-side Fraud Examiner.

**Lead with:** investigation planning, interviews and statements, claim-file review when supported, fraud indicators, evidence preservation, report writing, multi-jurisdiction coordination, restitution, and defensible handoff to counsel or prosecutors.

**Use carefully:** law-enforcement background as evidence of investigative judgment, not as a substitute for insurance knowledge. Be candid about insurance-specific tools or processes still being learned.

**Format:** conventional ATS resume, clear chronology, concise summary, core investigative competencies, and quantified case outcomes. Avoid a dense technical inventory unless the posting calls for it.

**Do not import:** customer adoption, QBR, account health, sales engineering, or broad cyber-threat language unless the posting requires it.

### 2. Corporate security and enterprise investigations

**Typical roles:** Corporate Security Investigator, Global Investigator, Insider Threat Investigator, Workplace Violence Investigator, Ethics or Enterprise Investigator.

**Lead with:** sensitive fact-finding, employee or insider matters, evidence handling, threat assessment, digital evidence, cross-functional work with Legal, HR, Internal Audit, and security leadership, and concise findings for decision-makers.

**Use carefully:** sworn experience as supporting credibility after the corporate risk frame is established.

**Format:** restrained executive style, risk and findings orientation, three or four thematic capabilities, and selected outcomes. Keep patrol terminology and criminal-procedure detail out unless directly relevant.

**Do not import:** policyholder language, carrier processes, product demos, pipeline terminology, or badge-forward framing.

### 3. Customer success management

**Typical roles:** Customer Success Manager, Senior CSM, Strategic CSM, Customer Enablement Manager, Agency Success Manager.

**Lead with:** customer adoption, relationship management, training, stakeholder communication, issue resolution, change management, retention-supporting work, customer outcomes, and the ability to translate complex products into useful workflows.

**Use carefully:** teaching, task-force coordination, agency relationships, real-estate client work, and end-user experience as evidence of customer empathy. Do not claim ownership of renewals, ARR, churn, NRR, QBRs, Salesforce, or a book of business unless verified.

**Format:** outcome-oriented ATS resume with a short customer-value summary, transferable achievements, and a relevant tools/technology section. Translate investigative work into customer, stakeholder, training, and adoption outcomes without disguising the underlying job.

**Do not import:** SIU claim terminology, criminal case chronology, or an oversized forensic tool list.

### 4. Technical account management

**Typical roles:** Technical Account Manager, Customer Success Engineer, Technical Customer Success Manager, Service Delivery Manager, implementation-oriented TAM.

**Lead with:** technical discovery, troubleshooting, escalation management, implementation support, workflow integration, customer training, product feedback, documentation, and coordination between users and technical teams.

**Use carefully:** hands-on forensic platforms, API automation, Python, evidence workflows, teaching, and multi-agency coordination. Distinguish direct technical use from formal SaaS account ownership.

**Format:** hybrid customer/technical ATS resume. Put technical capabilities near the top, but connect each tool to a customer or operational result. Include a concise transferable-experience statement when the title transition needs explanation.

**Do not import:** unverified cloud architecture, incident ownership, SLAs, ticket volumes, renewal quotas, or enterprise account metrics.

### 5. DFIR and cyber investigations

**Typical roles:** Digital Forensic Examiner, DFIR Investigator, Cybercrime Investigator, Cyber Fraud Analyst, Incident Response Investigator, forensic consultant.

**Lead with:** acquisition, preservation, examination, validation, chain of custody, mobile and computer forensics, forensic reporting, warrant or legal process, evidence volume, and support to technical and non-technical decision-makers.

**Use carefully:** name precise tools only at verified proficiency levels. Separate primary platforms from supplemental tools. State cyber or incident-response gaps plainly.

**Format:** technical ATS resume with an early tools/methods section, representative examinations or projects, evidence volumes, training, and certifications. Use exact technical nouns; avoid a generic leadership-heavy summary.

**Do not import:** CSM adoption language, insurance claim processes, or corporate risk language unless the position is hybrid.

### 6. Intelligence, fraud analytics, and cyber analysis

**Typical roles:** Intelligence Analyst, Financial Crime Analyst, Fraud Analyst, Threat Intelligence Analyst, Corporate Security Analyst.

**Lead with:** pattern recognition, OSINT, link analysis, written analytical products, briefing, fraud typologies, digital evidence, and explaining findings to non-technical audiences.

**Use carefully:** distinguish investigative experience from formal AML, SOC, threat-intelligence, or data-analytics experience. Do not inflate working familiarity into production expertise.

**Format:** analyst ATS resume with an analytical summary, methods/tools, selected written products or investigations, and education/training relevant to the posting.

**Do not import:** claim-adjuster language, customer-success metrics, or product-demo framing.

### 7. Solutions consulting and public-safety technology

**Typical roles:** Solutions Consultant, Sales Engineer, Public Safety Technology Advisor, Investigative Trainer, implementation consultant for investigative or public-safety technology.

**Lead with:** credible end-user perspective, technical demonstrations, discovery, training, workflow design, stakeholder communication, product feedback, and adoption.

**Use carefully:** do not claim sales quota, pipeline, solution architecture, or formal pre-sales ownership without evidence. Explain the transfer from operator and instructor experience.

**Format:** customer-facing technical resume with product experience, teaching/presentation evidence, multi-stakeholder work, and results relevant to implementation or adoption.

**Do not import:** SIU procedures or analyst tradecraft as the main identity.

## Hybrid-role rule

A posting may combine lanes, such as a DFIR vendor Technical Account Manager or a public-safety Customer Success Manager. Select the lane that owns the job's primary business outcome, then borrow only the relevant vocabulary and evidence from one secondary lane.

Examples:

- A Cellebrite TAM is primarily **technical account management**, secondarily **DFIR**.
- A Flock Safety CSM is primarily **customer success**, secondarily **public-safety solutions**.
- A carrier cyber-SIU role is primarily **SIU**, secondarily **DFIR/cyber**.
- An insider-threat analyst is primarily **corporate security** or **intelligence**, based on whether the output is an investigation or an analytical product.

Never blend every lane into one document.

## Formatting autonomy

The primary upload must be ATS-readable, but it does not have to use a single visual template. Follow `RESUME_HEADLINE_SCANABILITY_STANDARD.md` for the headline decision, opening-summary density, length, treatment of older experience, and verified metric placement.

The AI may select:

- A plain text-first ATS header for portals and automated parsing.
- The navy/gold branded header for a recruiter-facing PDF, networking copy, interview packet, or human-facing attachment.
- Chronological, hybrid chronological, or selected-achievements emphasis, provided the work history remains truthful and easy to follow.
- One or two pages based on role seniority and relevance.

Avoid columns, text boxes, graphics, ratings, photographs, and decorative skill bars in the primary ATS resume.

## Cover-letter autonomy

A cover letter should answer three questions in the language of the target industry:

1. Why this work and employer?
2. Which verified experiences best predict success?
3. What transition, gap, or unusual strength needs useful context?

The AI may choose the opener, evidence, paragraph order, and professional closing. It should not repeat the resume, force law-enforcement and military credentials into the first sentence, or insert a case statistic merely to satisfy a template.

## Decision rule

When a repository rule conflicts with this standard on document wording, ordering, identity, or visual presentation, this role-adaptation standard controls. Privacy, truth, factual verification, and PTSD-scope hard blocks always remain controlling.
