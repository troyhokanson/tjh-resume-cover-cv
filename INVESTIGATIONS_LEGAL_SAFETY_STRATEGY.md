## What makes a legal opening worth pursuing

Prioritize employers explicitly accepting law-enforcement, investigation, equivalent education/experience, or related casework. Favor substantial evidence review, fact development, public-records research, witness/client interviews, case preparation and attorney support. Consider plaintiff, defense, government and regulatory legal teams on the actual duties and requirements. Primarily receptionist, billing or calendar-focused roles receive lower priority when the work makes little use of this background.

Required qualifications control eligibility. Preferred qualifications are gaps to discuss, not automatic rejection. Do not infer a legal credential from investigation experience. Use the existing adaptive writing workflow to select an evidence-backed profile; never force legal or municipal safety roles into a vendor-sales profile.
