# Resume Headline and Scanability Standard

**Purpose:** Make Troy Hokanson's resumes immediately understandable to recruiters and ATS systems without sacrificing truth, relevance, or readability.

This standard applies to non-federal resumes, job-board profiles, recruiter summaries, and candidate profiles. Federal resumes follow the applicable announcement and federal format.

## 1. Headline decision

A one-line, role-aligned headline is the default for non-federal resumes. It appears directly below the name/contact block and before the professional summary.

The headline must:

- state a supported professional lane or capability set;
- use language that fits the target posting;
- remain one concise line and no more than 120 characters;
- avoid claiming that a target title is Troy's current job;
- avoid credentials, tools, or specialties that are not verified; and
- be adapted for targeted applications rather than copied universally.

A headline may be omitted when a portal, form, federal convention, or other document type makes it inappropriate. Record the reason when omitted.

Default general headline:

`Investigations and Digital Forensics | Fraud | Public Safety Technology`

Approved lane examples:

- SIU: `Fraud and SIU Investigations | Digital Evidence | Complex Case Development`
- Corporate investigations: `Corporate Investigations | Digital Evidence | Risk and Findings`
- DFIR: `Digital Forensic Examination | Mobile and Computer Evidence | Reporting`
- Intelligence: `Investigative Intelligence | OSINT | Fraud and Digital Evidence`
- Public-safety customer success: `Public Safety Technology | Customer Adoption | Training and Investigative Workflows`
- Technical account work: `Technical Account Support | Public Safety Technology | Digital Evidence Workflows`

Examples are starting points, not mandatory wording.

## 2. Opening summary

Use a short, scannable opening: normally two to four lines of prose or no more than three concise bullets. The summary must answer what Troy does, where his strongest verified evidence lies, and why it matters for the target role.

Do not require bullets when a compact paragraph reads more naturally. Do not repeat the headline or list every career lane.

## 3. Length and readability

One or two pages are acceptable for a private-sector resume. For Troy's experience level, two pages are the normal ceiling when both pages contain relevant evidence.

- Choose length based on relevance, seniority, and readable density.
- Do not force a one-page resume by shrinking body text or margins below approved minimums.
- Federal resumes and requested CVs are exempt from the two-page ceiling.
- Edit, consolidate, or remove low-value content before compressing typography.

## 4. Recency and earlier experience

Give the greatest detail to recent and target-relevant work. As a working guide, the most recent 10–15 years should receive the most space.

Do not delete experience solely because it is older. Compress older relevant work into an `Earlier Experience` or `Earlier Public-Safety Experience` block when it supplies useful evidence such as leadership, training, technology implementation, military service, or a role-specific accomplishment.

## 5. Metrics and bullet construction

- Use only verified, meaningful numbers.
- Place a high-value metric near the start of a selected bullet when it improves scanning.
- Do not force a number into every role.
- Do not invent percentages, volumes, savings, time reductions, customer outcomes, or proxy metrics.
- Use the most specific accurate verb. Avoid obvious adjacent repetition, but never substitute a less accurate verb merely for variety.
- Prefer outcome, scale, and decision value over routine duty lists.

## 6. ATS structure

The primary portal version must use:

- a single-column, text-first layout;
- standard section headings;
- contact information in the document body rather than only in a Word header;
- no tables, text boxes, decorative skill bars, ratings, photographs, or essential information carried only by graphics; and
- natural keyword alignment supported by Troy's verified record.

Do not keyword-stuff. ATS targeting is a relevance and wording exercise, not a license to add unsupported claims.

## 7. Review-source rule

External resume scores and commercial critiques are diagnostic inputs, not governing facts. Adopt recommendations that improve clarity, relevance, and truthful evidence. When a review is contradictory, promotional, or unsupported, verify the underlying issue before changing the resume or a durable standard.

## 8. Final gate

Before delivery, confirm:

- headline included or omission rationale recorded;
- summary length and scanability passed;
- verified metrics prioritized where natural;
- older work compressed by relevance rather than age alone;
- ATS structure passed;
- factual chronology and credential status match current constants; and
- every rendered page passed visual inspection.
