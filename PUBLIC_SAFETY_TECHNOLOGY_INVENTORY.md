## 1. Purpose

This file records Troy's documented public-safety technology experience so future job-fit reviews, resumes, cover letters, interview preparation, and automated searches do not reduce his background to generic law-enforcement experience or overstate brief exposure.

For every public-safety, SLED, law-enforcement technology, digital-forensics, digital-evidence, ALPR, body-camera, fleet-video, communications, patrol-equipment, or DFR-related role, use this inventory to distinguish:

- **Primary workflow platform** — used routinely as a principal tool during a defined period or for a defined function.
- **Substantial direct use** — repeated hands-on use in real patrol, investigative, evidence, training, or forensic work.
- **Supplemental or backup platform** — used selectively when the primary tool did not recover, parse, organize, or present evidence adequately, or retained as a secondary option.
- **Agency-side project involvement** — participated in evaluation, acquisition, implementation, rollout, or workflow adoption from the customer side.
- **Brief direct exposure** — personally used, but not long enough to claim advanced proficiency.
- **Adjacent awareness** — understands the operational environment or current agency adoption but lacks direct hands-on use.

Never convert end-user experience into vendor-employment experience. Never convert brief exposure into expertise. Never flatten tools used at different stages of Troy's career into a false claim that all platforms were used equally.

## 3. Experience-Level Labels

Use these labels internally when selecting resume content:

| Label | Meaning | Permitted wording |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Primary platform** | Principal tool for a defined period or workflow | `primary platform`, `principal examination tool`, `routine workflow` |
| **D1 — Substantial direct use** | Repeated hands-on use in operational or investigative work | `used`, `operated`, `conducted examinations with`, `worked cases using` |
| **D2 — Direct operational use** | Personally used in normal duty workflows | `used`, `operated`, `worked with` |
| **S1 — Supplemental or backup use** | Used selectively or retained as a secondary option | `supplemented with`, `used selectively`, `retained as a backup` |
| **D3 — Brief direct exposure** | Personally used for a limited period | `brief hands-on exposure`, `operator familiarity`, `used briefly before rotating out` |
| **A1 — Agency-side project involvement** | Participated as the customer/end-user organization in implementation or adoption | `agency-side project experience`, `worked with the vendor`, `supported rollout`, `helped integrate into workflow` |
| **C1 — Adjacent/current awareness** | Understands the use case or local adoption but did not personally operate it | `developing domain knowledge`, `agency-adoption awareness`, `operationally informed` |
